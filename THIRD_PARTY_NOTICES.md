The BSD-3-Clause license applies to the contributors' original code in this
archive. Existing third-party copyright and license notices remain applicable
to any third-party components. Imported libraries and externally downloaded
models retain their own licenses and terms. No model weights are redistributed
by this archive.

The code license does not grant rights to the datasets or manuscript figures.
NYISO records remain subject to the provider's applicable terms. Zhejiang
records, including the candidate demonstration week, retain the data holder's
access and redistribution restrictions. See README.md and the source manifest.

The tested workflow uses NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn and Matplotlib installed
from their upstream distributions. Original predictive implementations can
require PyTorch, TabPFN, Chronos and other packages listed in
docs/archived_implementations.md. Preserve upstream notices when redistributing
these dependencies or their assets.
